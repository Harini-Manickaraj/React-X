WHAT REACT-X PROVIDES

Everything your on-call team needs

From the first alert to full post-mortem — REACT-X handles every step of the incident lifecycle.

Real-Time Incident Detection

Live signals stream new incidents to your dashboard the moment they are detected — no manual polling needed. Critical alerts trigger instant notifications.

AI-Powered Correlation

REACT-X correlates alerts across services using topology, time-series, and semantic signals — collapsing alert storms into a single prioritised incident.

Deep Root Cause Analysis

Evidence collection, root cause identification, blast radius, impact scoring, and risk assessment — all surfaced automatically with every investigation.

OCR Screenshot Import

Upload any error screenshot directly. REACT-X extracts the text using OCR, auto-detects severity, and pre-fills the incident form for fast creation.

Step-by-Step Resolution

Every incident gets a concrete numbered action plan. Approve the fix, add a resolution note, and REACT-X calculates MTTR and moves it to history automatically.

Live Dashboard & Analytics

KPI cards for open/critical/investigating counts, severity breakdown charts, type distribution, 7-day volume trends — and a high-risk alert notification panel.

Full Audit & History

Every resolved incident is logged with MTTR, resolution notes, and timestamps. Full-text search, severity filter, and one-click CSV export for compliance.

High-Risk Alert Notifications

Critical and high-severity incidents are surfaced in a dedicated notification panel on the dashboard. One click opens the full investigation queue.

HOW IT WORKS

The complete incident lifecycle

REACT-X follows a closed-loop process — if resolution fails the verification check, the cycle restarts automatically.

1
🔍

Detect

Alerts stream in via live signals, manual entry, or OCR screenshot import. Critical incidents are flagged immediately.

→
2
🔗

Correlate

AI groups related alerts from multiple services into a single incident cluster, eliminating noise and duplicates.

→
3
🧪

Investigate

Evidence is gathered, root cause identified, blast radius mapped, impact scored, and risk assessed automatically.

→
4
⚖️

Decide

Your team reviews the AI-generated analysis and chooses from a ranked list of resolution actions with risk scores.

→
5
✅

Approve

An authorised team member approves the selected action plan. The step-by-step resolution guide is unlocked.

→
6
⚡

Resolve

Follow the numbered steps, add a resolution note, and mark the incident as complete. MTTR is calculated instantly.

→
7
🔄

Verify

REACT-X checks whether the incident is truly resolved. If it recurs or metrics don't recover, the loop restarts from Step 1.

If verification fails — the incident is reopened and the full cycle repeats until confirmed resolved.
